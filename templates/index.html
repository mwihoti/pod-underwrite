Why a seller would pay for this

A circuit that passes a spot check and fails one vector can still be sold: the buyer finds out when the challenge lands, not when they wire the money. This gives the same answer a challenger would compute, at a pinned block, in under a minute, with the counterexample.

One processor, one seat, one winner per task: on PoD a correct-but-slower design mines at bonus zero. The audit reports C against the seat's best so a buyer sees that before paying, not after.
