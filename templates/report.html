Read this before you rely on it

  • Combinational tasks are compared against all 256 official vectors, which are the protocol's own correctness surface. That is not the same claim as "correct on every input" — only the exhaustive mode (reference netlist supplied) says that.
  • Sequential tasks are sampled. A clean sample is reported as a sample.
  • The netlist is evaluated by our independent simulator, not by the chain. The cost figure is ours; where the protocol's own verifier answered, both numbers are shown.
  • A verdict is about this netlist bytes at this task. It says nothing about who owns the circuit or whether it holds the seat tomorrow.
